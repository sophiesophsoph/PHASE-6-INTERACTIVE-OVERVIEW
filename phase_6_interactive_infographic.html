Phase 6 Overview Rule

Evaluation & Lesson Revision Framework

Evaluation covers three key areas: pupils' achievement of each objective, the effectiveness of methods and media, and decisions for revising the lesson.

💡 Crucial Note: Achievement is judged strictly from individual evidence. Group observation judges participation and cooperation, and group results are not used to assume that every member has mastered the objectives.
🎯

6.2 Speaking Rubric

Aligned to DSKP Performance Levels (1 to 6)

Criterion Levels 1–2 (Needs Support) Levels 3–4 (Secure - Target) Levels 5–6 (Confident)
🔄 Keeping the interaction going Needs teacher or peer prompts; fewer than 3 exchanges Keeps going for at least 4 exchanges with the Phrase Bank Keeps going independently for 5 or more exchanges and adds extra turns
❓ Suitable questions Repeats questions only after prompting Asks or answers at least 1 suitable question ("Would you like...?" "Anything else?") Asks 2 or more suitable questions and answers naturally
💬 Key phrases Uses 1–3 key phrases with errors Uses at least 4 key phrases accurately Uses key phrases accurately and adds an opinion or reason
📢 Pronunciation of menu words Many words unclear At least 8 of 10 words clear; /ɪ/ and /iː/ mostly distinguished All words clear with confident stress and intonation

6.3 Wayground Quiz Blueprint

8 Items (Individual Pupil Assessment)

Interactive Item Filter
Filter by Objective:
Item 1 Obj: O2

Listening, Multiple Choice

Hear a price (for example 'one pound twenty') and choose the correct number.

Item 2 Obj: O2

Listening, Multiple Choice

Hear a price (for example 'four pounds ninety-five') and choose the correct number.

Item 3 Obj: O2

Listening, Picture Choice

Hear a short order and choose the matching pictures. 🥪🧃

Item 4 Obj: O2

Listening, Multiple Choice

Hear an order with prices and choose the correct total calculation.

Item 5 Obj: O2

Listening, Gap-Fill Choice

Hear a waiter's line and choose the missing key phrase ('Would you ... salad with that?').

Item 6 Obj: O1

Sound Recognition

Which word has the same vowel sound as 'cheese'? 🧀 (meat / chips / fish)

Item 7 Obj: O1

Picture to Word Match

Match the picture to the word (for example vegetarian burger 🍔).

Item 8 Obj: O1 / O3 support

Polite Reply

Choose the polite reply to 'Anything else?'.

📌 Pedagogical Alignment: Quiz items use the same menu words and prices practised in the lesson, so that the quiz tests what was taught. The teacher checks the answer key before use.

6.5 Evaluating Effectiveness of Methods & Media

Six Key Evaluation Aspects

💻 Learning Value of Technology

Evaluation Question: Did the menu board, pictures and audio help pupils recognise menu words and distinguish /ɪ/ and /iː/?

Gathered via: Pronunciation checklist; Wayground item analysis for items 6–8

💬 Interaction

Evaluation Question: Did the role cards and digital menu help all pupils speak, or did some pupils dominate?

Gathered via: Observation of talk time per role; rubric records

⏱️ Time

Evaluation Question: Did each stage take the planned time?

Gathered via: Teacher notes with actual timings

📱 Access and Display

Evaluation Question: Did devices, audio and QR codes work? Could all pupils see and hear?

Gathered via: Teacher log of problems and the workaround used

🙋 Pupil Voice

Evaluation Question: Which part of the lesson helped most?

Gathered via: Three pupils asked at the exit counter; traffic-light results

🔒 Ethics and Safety

Evaluation Question: Were privacy and fairness protected?

Gathered via: Check that nicknames were used, no personal data was collected and recordings (if any) were deleted

6.6 Revision Decisions

If Evidence Shows... Then Teacher Will...

If Evidence Shows:

Many pupils mispronounce the same words (for example cheese as chips)

Then the Teacher Will:

Change the examples, add minimal-pair practice and a short pronunciation game in the next lesson.

If Evidence Shows:

Many pupils miss the total in the listening items

Then the Teacher Will:

Replay shorter audio segments, show numbers after the second listening and add more price practice.

If Evidence Shows:

Role-play stays at one or two exchanges

Then the Teacher Will:

Add more sentence frames, extra situation cards and a stronger model conversation.

If Evidence Shows:

Time is not enough

Then the Teacher Will:

Shorten the digital demonstration and the second role-play round.

If Evidence Shows:

Access is interrupted

Then the Teacher Will:

Use printed equivalents and review the evidence again after remediation.

6.7 Remediation and Enrichment

Targeted Follow-up Activities & Progress Evidence

Need: Not yet mastered O1 🎴

Small-Group Picture Practice

Small-group practice with six picture cards; repeat the menu words and the /ɪ/ and /iː/ minimal pairs; increase the number of cards gradually.

Evidence of Progress

Pupil says at least 8 of 10 words clearly in a re-check.

Need: Not yet mastered O2 🎧

Segmented Audio & Price Writing

Re-listen to short dialogues in segments with picture support; practise writing prices heard.

Evidence of Progress

Pupil answers at least 4 of 5 listening items in a new short quiz.

Need: Not yet mastered O3 🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Buddy Role-Play & Teacher Modelling

Role-play with sentence frames and a buddy; teacher takes the waiter role to model suitable questions.

Evidence of Progress

Pupil keeps a conversation going for at least 4 exchanges.

Need: Mastered all objectives ⭐

Enrichment: 'Special of the Day'

Create a 'special of the day' with a price, say why it is healthy and present it to the class. 🥗

Evidence of Progress

Pupil links food, price and a reason accurately.
